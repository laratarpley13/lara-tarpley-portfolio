About me.

What drove my transition from Software Engineering to Data Science was the realization that I wasn't just building a product—I was building a system to generate and interpret the true story of our business.

I'm excited to apply advanced statistical modeling and machine learning to move beyond reporting and deliver truly predictive, data-driven solutions. I'm ready to turn data into a competitive advantage.

When I am not running a model, you will most likely find me in the kitchen attempting to develop new recipes and dishes to share with my family or enjoying the outdoors, whether that is a nice hike or reading a good book at a park.
